
### **73. Becoming Infinite**

*How transcendence hides in the ordinary*

Transcendence is often imagined as extraordinary — mystical experiences, monumental achievements, or glimpses of the divine. Yet the infinite quietly resides in the ordinary, in the small, everyday moments where presence, attention, and awareness intersect. A shared conversation, the stillness of dawn, the act of creation — these are the spaces where the self dissolves into connection, and the ordinary becomes boundless.

Becoming infinite is not about escaping mortality, but about expanding perception. When we fully inhabit experience, noticing subtleties, patterns, and connections, we touch something larger than ourselves. Time feels elastic, identity softens, and the self participates in a continuity that exceeds individual existence. The ordinary, lived fully, becomes a portal to the eternal.

This practice is subtle, requiring no ritual or grand gesture, only attentive presence. In acts of love, care, reflection, or creation, we glimpse infinity. By valuing depth over spectacle, engagement over accumulation, and connection over isolation, we cultivate moments where life feels limitless, even within finite boundaries.

Ultimately, becoming infinite teaches that transcendence is not somewhere else; it is woven into the texture of ordinary life. By embracing presence, awareness, and connection, we discover that the eternal is always accessible — hiding in plain sight, waiting for recognition in the rhythms, choices, and experiences of our everyday existence.
