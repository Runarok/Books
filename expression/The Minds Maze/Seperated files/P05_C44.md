### **44. Inherited Dreams**

*Goals that aren’t yours but live in you*

Sometimes, the ambitions we chase aren’t born from our own desires but from the expectations, stories, and dreams of others. Parents, mentors, culture, or society can plant visions of what a “successful” life looks like, and over time, we absorb them so deeply that they feel like our own. These inherited dreams can provide direction, motivation, and opportunity, yet they can also create tension between what feels authentic and what feels expected.

Living with inherited dreams often brings a subtle dissonance. You might achieve milestones, gain recognition, or follow a path of excellence, yet feel a quiet emptiness or sense that something is missing. The origin of ambition matters: is it truly yours, or a borrowed compass guiding your steps? Recognizing this difference is essential for aligning life with personal values rather than inherited narratives.

Awareness of inherited dreams allows conscious choice. You can honor what resonates, modify what feels imposed, or discard what constrains. This process is not about rejecting guidance or legacy but about distinguishing between authentic purpose and conditioned expectation.

By examining inherited dreams, you reclaim authorship of your life. You step out from scripts written by others and move toward goals that carry personal meaning, cultivating a sense of fulfillment that is grounded in your own values, passions, and choices.
