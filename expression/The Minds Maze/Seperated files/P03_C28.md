### **28. Thresholds**

*Moments that split life into “before” and “after”*

Some moments are invisible at first, yet later reveal themselves as turning points — thresholds that divide life into “before” and “after.” They may be sudden or subtle, dramatic or quiet, but their impact reshapes perception, priorities, and the sense of who you are. Crossing a threshold is rarely obvious in the moment; only in hindsight do we see how everything shifted.

These moments can be joyful or painful: leaving a place, meeting someone who changes your path, enduring loss, or making a choice that alters the course of life. They are markers of transformation, punctuating the flow of ordinary days with experiences that demand reflection and adjustment. Thresholds are both endings and beginnings, liminal spaces where the past dissolves and the future emerges.

Recognizing thresholds helps you understand the rhythm of change. Life is rarely smooth or predictable; it is composed of these turning points that force adaptation, growth, and reevaluation. Some thresholds bring clarity, others confusion, but all invite presence — the awareness to notice that something has shifted and that you are moving into new territory.

By seeing the significance of these moments, you can navigate transitions with more consciousness and intention. Thresholds are reminders that life is not a straight line, but a series of passages, each shaping the story of who you are becoming.
