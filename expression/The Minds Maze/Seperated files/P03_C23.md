### **23. The Clock Inside You**

*Biological vs. psychological time*

Time is never just the ticking of a clock. Within you, there are multiple rhythms — the biological and the psychological — each moving at its own pace and shaping your experience in ways that often go unnoticed. Biological time governs your body: the heartbeat, the sleep cycle, the seasons of growth and decay. Psychological time, by contrast, is the inner sense of how fast or slow life passes, colored by memory, attention, and emotion.

A single hour can feel endless when waiting for news, yet fly by unnoticed when immersed in passion or flow. Days can blur into years, and milestones arrive in a mix of anticipation and surprise. These internal clocks influence not only how you perceive life but how you act, plan, and respond to change. They create tension between urgency and patience, between instinct and reflection.

Understanding the clocks inside you is not about trying to synchronize perfectly with the world around you. It is about noticing your rhythms, respecting the pace your body and mind require, and realizing how perception shapes reality. What feels “late” may be perfectly on time for your internal world, and what feels “fast” may carry lessons hidden in its brevity.

By attuning to both biological and psychological time, you can move through life with greater awareness and gentleness. You begin to see that life’s timing is not only measured in minutes and years, but also in the moments that stretch, compress, and resonate uniquely within you.
