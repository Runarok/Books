### **21. The Sliding Doors**

*Alternate lives you never lived*

Every choice you make opens a door — and every door you don’t take leaves another life behind. These are the “sliding doors” of existence: the paths you might have walked but didn’t, the moments where a different decision could have led to a completely different you. They are invisible yet persistent, echoing in your imagination and sometimes in quiet, unspoken regret.

These alternate lives are not just fantasies; they are reminders of possibility. They show how fragile the line between who you are and who you might have been can be. A single decision — leaving a city, speaking a truth, staying silent, choosing one job over another — can create an entirely new trajectory. And while some paths would have brought joy, others might have brought pain, so these “what ifs” are never purely comforting or purely threatening.

Sliding doors also reveal the illusion of control. We often imagine that a single choice determines everything, but life is a web of interactions, timing, and circumstances. Still, noticing these alternate lives teaches perspective. It reminds you that life is a series of intersections, that some choices close doors forever, and that your current path — for better or worse — is built from countless unseen possibilities.

Reflecting on the lives you didn’t live isn’t about regret; it’s about awareness. It’s about seeing the complexity of your journey, honoring the paths you did take, and understanding that every decision carries both loss and gain. The sliding doors exist as quiet mirrors, showing that even the life you inhabit now is shaped as much by the roads you didn’t choose as by the ones you did.
