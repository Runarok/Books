## **Preface**

This book started as a personal attempt to **understand myself better**.
I wanted to explore my thoughts and feelings about **love, hate, trust, standards, and clarity** — not to give answers, but to figure out what I actually believe.

While writing, I realized something important:
Most people **act like they understand these concepts**, but very few actually do. There’s a difference between **knowing the words** and **living the meaning**.

This isn’t a guide or a set of rules.
It’s more like a **collection of reflections** — thoughts shaped by observation, self-exploration, and adapting to the world around me.

If you read this, don’t take it as advice.
Think of it as **a mirror**: maybe some of these ideas will resonate with you, maybe they won’t. And that’s fine.

I didn’t write this to teach anyone.
I wrote it to **understand myself**, and maybe, in the process, it’ll help you reflect on your own perspectives too.
